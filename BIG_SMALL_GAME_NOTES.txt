BIG SMALL / WINGO GAME NOTES

- Existing result-selection and settlement rules are preserved.
- Supported independent round durations: 30 seconds, 1 minute, 3 minutes, and 5 minutes.
- Each duration has its own round clock, round identifiers, pending bets, settlement, results history, and personal bet history.
- Betting closes during the final 5 seconds of each selected duration.
- The UI duration tabs switch the selected mode and update the countdown, round label, and displayed history.
- Results remain server-generated and are saved using the existing persistence mechanism.
